Two ways to begin.

Both of them end in the same conversation with me. The only question is whether you want to tell me about the business first, or work it out together on the call.

Tell me first

The more I know about you, the easier our conversation is. About twenty minutes, saved as you go, so you can stop and come back. Then you book the call at the end.

Don’t worry about the questions you can’t answer — leave them, tick ask me on the call, and we talk those through together instead. A half-finished brief is still a better start than a blank one.

I’m already trading I’m just starting out

Two versions of the same brief. Pick whichever describes you now — it only changes the wording of the questions.

Talk it through instead

I’d rather just get you on the phone. Straight to my calendar, no forms, nothing to prepare. We cover the same ground out loud.

Pick this one if you’d sooner explain it than type it, or if you want to know whether I’m the right person before you spend twenty minutes on anything.

Book the call now

Free, and you’ll be told plainly if we’re not a fit.

Neither one commits you to anything. The brief costs you nothing and buys you a call spent on what to actually do, rather than on me asking what you sell. If you’d rather keep it short, take the second door — it is a real option, not a fallback.
