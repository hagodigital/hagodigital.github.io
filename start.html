Two ways to begin.

Both end in the same conversation. The only question is whether you tell me about the business first, or we work it out together on the call.

However you start

Neither one commits you to anything.

01

Tell me about the business first

The more I know, the better the call.

A short brief about what you sell, who buys it and where you stand. It costs you nothing, and it buys you a call spent on what to actually do — instead of on me asking what you sell.

  • About twenty minutes, and it saves as you go
  • Anything you’re unsure of — leave it, and we do it together
  • You book the call at the end, with me already up to speed

A half-finished brief is still a better start than a blank one. Nobody is marked on it.

I’m already trading I’m just starting out

Two versions of the same brief — it only changes the wording of the questions.

02

Or talk it through instead

Straight to the diary. Nothing to prepare.

Skip the brief and pick a time. We cover the same ground out loud, and if something needs writing down afterwards, I write it down.

  • Half an hour, free, no pitch deck
  • Better if you’d sooner explain it than type it
  • If we’re not a fit, you’ll be told plainly

You can always fill the brief in later — nothing here is a one-way door.
