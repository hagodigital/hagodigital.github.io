Know the difference

Brand, branding, brand identity — three different things

Brand

The reputation
  • The perception and gut feeling customers already have about you.
  • Built by experiences, conversations and outcomes.
  • What people say about you when you’re not in the room.

Branding

The action
  • The deliberate steps you take to shape that perception.
  • How you communicate, show up and deliver on the promise.
  • Ongoing, not a one-off.

Brand identity

The expression
  • The visual and verbal toolkit that brings the brand to life.
  • Logo, colour, typography, imagery and patterns.
  • What customers see, hear and recognise instantly.

Together they turn a business into a recognisable, trusted and preferred choice — instant recognition, deeper loyalty, and daylight between you and everyone else in a crowded market.
