Know the difference

Brand, branding, brand identity — three different things

Most people use the three words for one thing. Knowing which you actually need is what decides the work.

Brand

The reputation
  • What people think and feel about your business.
  • Shaped by their experiences with you.
  • Earned through what you do.

Branding

The action
  • The choices you make to shape that reputation.
  • How you communicate and deliver.
  • An ongoing part of running the business.

Brand identity

The expression
  • The visual and verbal tools you use.
  • Logo, colours, typography and voice.
  • A practical foundation for how you present yourself.

We build the identity. Your reputation grows through what your business does with it.
