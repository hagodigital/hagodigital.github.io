The gap

You’re not losing to better work.
You’re losing to better evidence.

Nobody can judge your work before they hire you. So they judge what they can see — a listing with three photos, a site from four years ago, a name they can’t tell apart from the four other tabs they’ve got open. They’re finding you. They’re reading you. Then they’re calling someone else, and you never hear about it.

Everyone’s got something tidy now. Looking sorted gets you in the running. It won’t get you chosen.

The part nobody explains

Three words the industry uses like they’re the same thing.

They’re not. And the difference is where your money is.

01

Your brand

What people say when you’re not in the room. You’ve already got one — you just didn’t pick it. It got built out of scraps. How the phone gets answered. That review from 2022.

You don’t own your brand. Your customers do. Right now it’s two words. “He’s good.” So is everyone else’s.

02

Your positioning

Deciding what those words should be instead. The sparky who tells you when you don’t need the job.

It has to survive being repeated by someone who wasn’t really listening. Get it right and you’re being sold in your sleep.

03

Your branding

Everything that makes it stick. Logo, colours, type, the site, the listing. Also the van, the quote, and what happens when something goes wrong.

Not design. Every signal you send.

Decide it → Build it → Put it where they’re looking. That order. Build the logo first and you get something expensive that says nothing.

What I do

01 — Get found

Your Google profile

How they find you on Maps and Search. Reviews, photos, one tap to call. People searching aren’t browsing — they need it done today. Free, usually half-built, and the fastest win you’ve got.

Your brand

A tidy profile makes you look legitimate. A brand makes you look like the obvious one. What you’re known for, and the system that makes it recognisable — logo suite, colours, type, a graphic language built from your actual work, custom icons, and one signature asset chosen for you, not stamped on you.

Drawn by a person, by hand, in vector — so nobody can take it off you.

03 — Get contacted

Your website

Where everything lands — Google, socials, referrals, the bloke who wrote your name on a bit of paper. Fast, hand-built, no monthly hosting bill.

It answers the questions you’re up a ladder to answer, and turns a look into a phone call. An enquiry hits your phone the second it’s sent.

Every site ships search-ready — titles, headings, alt text, schema, sitemap, Search Console in your account. That’s a foundation, not a ranking promise. Nobody can honestly sell you rankings; what gets you found locally is your profile and your reviews, which is why that’s in the build rather than sold to you as a mystery.
