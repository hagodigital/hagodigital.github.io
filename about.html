Why HagoDigital exists

I started HagoDigital after noticing the same pattern: good businesses were losing out because people couldn’t see what made them worth choosing.

Hago is Spanish for “I make”. For us, that means taking responsibility for what we put into the world.

Marketing without the theatre

What interests me about marketing is the practical challenge underneath it: helping people understand the value in a business without exaggeration or tricks.

I’d rather point to something we can improve than make you feel out of your depth.

Why creativity is not decoration

I don’t see creativity as decoration. The words, colours and details you choose change how a business comes across. We make those decisions deliberately, so the finished work feels like it belongs to you.

Founder-led. Lean by design

We work with you directly, with specialists involved where the work calls for their craft.

I stay responsible for the strategy, the message and the final judgement. We make clear who is doing what.
