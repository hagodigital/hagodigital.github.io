I started HagoDigital because of a pattern I kept noticing: the best business in a market is very often not the one getting the work. Someone with real skill, a decade of it, losing jobs to a competitor who is simply easier to find and easier to trust at a glance. That gap is unfair, and it’s fixable, and closing it is a genuinely interesting problem.

Hago is Spanish for “I make.” There’s a difference between consuming and making — consuming takes what already exists; making is the slower business of building something that wasn’t there before. That’s the difference I want this to be built on.

It’s also why I tend to get on with the people I work with: you’re a maker too. You didn’t just imagine a business, you started one, and most people never get that far. So we start on common ground — you made something worth choosing, and my job is making sure the right people can tell.

Marketing has been a long-running interest rather than a career I fell into. What I find compelling is the honest version of the problem: how a good business earns attention, trust, and a decision, without exaggerating and without tricks. I’d rather show you one specific thing that’s wrong with your Google listing than tell you that you don’t know what you don’t know.

Every project is led by me, and I stay close to the strategy, the message and the direction from the first conversation to the final handover — so there is one clear point of responsibility and you always know who you are talking to. The making is shared. Your logo is drawn by a professional graphic designer, and where a job needs a specialist you are told plainly who is doing what.

That is deliberate, and it is also the reason to pick us. Your website is our portfolio and your Google listing is our reference, so we only win when you do. You don’t need someone who overpromises and underdelivers. You need someone who treats your business as though their reputation depends on it. Ours does.

— JP, founder
