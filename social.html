Social media, handled

Everything else gets finished. This one keeps going

A brand, a website and a profile are built once. A social account is only ever as current as the last thing on it — which is why this line runs monthly.

01 · The plan

Know what you’re posting — and why

What do people need to understand before they choose you? We build your content plan around that: explaining your services, answering common questions and showing what makes your business worth considering.

Our social media management services cover the plan, the content and the publishing — the whole of what we take on as a social media management agency.

  • A clear audience and purpose for your content
  • Content themes drawn from your business, expertise and work
  • A monthly calendar you approve before anything goes out

What we commit to is the work: the plan, the content and the publishing. Nobody can promise you reach or followers, and the platforms change the rules without telling anyone.

02 · The content

Make every post recognisably yours

Your content should look and sound like your business. We use your colours, fonts and tone of voice to create a consistent presence across your posts and profiles.

  • Branded posts, carousels and short-form video
  • Captions written in your business’s voice
  • Profile bios and links that make the next step clear

We focus on Instagram, with LinkedIn and X where they suit your audience and the agreed plan.

03 · Publishing and review

Approved by you. Taken care of by us

Once you approve the month’s content, we schedule and publish it. At the end of the month, you get a plain-language review so you can see what happened and understand what comes next.

  • Scheduling and publishing handled for you
  • A clear review of what went out and how people responded
  • Recommendations for the following month

A clear monthly plan. Your accounts and content stay yours.

The service runs monthly, with one month’s notice to stop. Your accounts stay in your name, and you keep every file we create.

Why it matters

When they’re ready, be the business they already know

Not everyone who finds you is ready to enquire. Consistent, relevant content gives them a way to get to know what you do, see the value in it, and remember you when the timing is right.
